BRAND · BURBUR
/ˈbɜr·bɜr/ · bur-bur The sound lips make when a breath of air flaps them open. Nobody decides to make it.
bubble hand letteringoriginal · drawn as lines, no font file
Upright, one line weight, round ends. Dots on i, j, ! and ? are tiny open bubbles; a, d, l, t, u end in a small flick; g, j, y end in an open hook; letters bounce a hair on the baseline.
Rules • The wordmark is always lowercase burbur in the bubble hand. In running text write Burbur. • No Chinese name, no translation, no slant. • Shapes stay circles and pills, never rounded squares. Name check: no existing app called Burbur found. It is close in spelling to Burberry, which defends its name, so get a trademark search before launch.
